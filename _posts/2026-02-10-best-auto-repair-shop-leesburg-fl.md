Finding a mechanic you can trust is one of those things that seems hard until you find the right shop — then you never go anywhere else. If you're in Leesburg or anywhere in Lake County and looking for reliable auto repair, here's what actually matters.

## What Makes a Good Auto Repair Shop

**Honest diagnosis before any work**
The most important thing a mechanic can do is tell you the truth. That means diagnosing the actual problem — not guessing, not recommending unnecessary work, and not padding the repair order with things you don't need. A good shop shows you what's wrong and explains it clearly before touching anything.

**Fair, upfront pricing**
Trustworthy shops give you a clear estimate before starting work. Surprises on the final bill are a red flag. You should know what you're paying before you approve the repair.

**Experience with your type of vehicle**
Not every shop is equally comfortable with every vehicle. If you drive a Range Rover, a diesel truck, or a European import, make sure the shop has experience with it — not just generic experience.

**A track record in the community**
Google reviews from real local customers tell you a lot. Look for patterns: do reviewers mention honesty, fair pricing, quality work? Do they come back? Are they referring friends and family? That's the real signal.

**Someone who treats your car like it matters**
You can usually tell within the first five minutes of talking to a mechanic whether they're actually paying attention to your problem or just going through the motions.

## What to Watch Out For

- Shops that diagnose over the phone without seeing the car
- Pressure to approve repairs immediately "before it gets worse"
- Vague estimates with no itemization
- Recommending the same major repair for every complaint
- No warranty on their work
